Open-Garage — Wiring Recipe 🍳

ESP32 → opto-isolated relay → garage opener's 12 V dry-contact button terminals. The relay momentarily "presses the button" for you when the web app sends a command.

Safety first Your opener terminals are low-voltage (NEC CL2, 12 V) — safe to touch. Only ever connect the relay's COM/NO across the two button terminals (red + white-left). Never wire the ESP32's power to those terminals, and never touch the mains/motor side. Unplug the opener while wiring.

2 · Wiring diagram

Why in parallel? You keep the existing wall button working. Each relay contact wire shares a terminal with the button wire via a lever nut — pressing the button or closing the relay both trigger the door.

3 · Step-by-step

  1. Bench test first (no garage yet). Power the ESP32 by USB. Wire ESP32 5V→VCC, GND→GND, GPIO4→IN. Leave COM/NO unconnected. You should hear the relay click each time a command arrives.
  2. Send a test command. From the web app press Activar (or we publish a test). The relay should click once. If it clicks on the wrong state (energized at rest), the module is active-low — tell me and I flip RELAY_ACTIVE_LOW and reflash.
  3. Unplug the garage opener at the wall before touching its terminals.
  4. Tap the two button terminals. At the RED terminal, use a lever nut to join the existing red button wire + one relay wire (COM). At the WHITE-left terminal, join the green button wire + the other relay wire (NO). Order/polarity doesn't matter — it's a dry contact.
  5. Power the opener back on. Press the physical wall button — door still works (proves you didn't break it).
  6. Trigger from the app. Press Activar → the relay closes ~0.4 s → door moves. 🎉
  7. Mount it. Put the ESP32 + relay in the enclosure, stick it near the opener, plug into the 5 V adapter. Confirm it's in WiFi range (serial shows wifi ok <ip>).

4 · Firmware settings (already flashed)

RELAY_PIN4 — matches GPIO4 above. Change here if you use another pin (avoid 6–11; 0/2/12/15 are boot-strapping pins).
RELAY_ACTIVE_LOWSet to true for most opto relay modules (IN=LOW closes). We confirm in step 2.
PULSE_MS400 ms button press. Fine for virtually all openers.

Reflash after any change:

arduino-cli compile --fqbn esp32:esp32:esp32:UploadSpeed=115200 -u -p /dev/ttyUSB0 firmware/garage
